**What the runs showed:**
- **No gross timing edge on either window.**
  - Every trend-only dose is negative at zero cost on the holdout (−0.40 to −1.91 pp).
  - On the August window only 360m is positive at zero cost, and only barely (+0.145 pp).
  - The dev baseline sits at ~0 gross on both windows.
  - Across five windows now, the trend family's gross edge has been +2.98, +2.06, −0.05/+0.76, and now −1.91 to −0.40 pp. This looks like noise around zero, not a stable edge.
  - The improvement over the dev baseline is almost entirely cost reduction.
- **Band edge + round-up works as intended on cost.**
  - At 500k, traded volume fell 66% vs the plain 120m reference, and fees fell 61% (10,182 → 3,946 JPY).
  - At 50k the round-up fired on 32 of 40 trades. That fixed the 2026-11-13 failure, where band-edge stopped trading at 50k.
  - It also cost the least gross value of any variant (−0.40 pp at zero cost vs −1.91 pp).
- **Round-up side effect.** All 11 round-ups at 500k were one-lot "top-ups" that fired when price drift pushed |delta| just past the threshold (0.153-0.172) right after a band-edge trade. A refinement would round up only when the band-edge size is > 0 and below the lot.
- **Slower trend periods beat plain 120m** (+1.58 pp net, +1.44 pp zero-cost) but do not change the overall picture.
- **360m cut fees by half vs 120m**, but on the holdout it lost more gross value than band-edge did.

## New hypotheses generated this run

- **trend-only-subsignals-360m-threshold-015** (algorithm): cost-side, literature-motivated (Bui & Nguyen, arXiv:2602.11708). This is the next spacing dose after 120m, at the H6 frequency the paper found best net of costs. Pros: same asset class and the same turnover-vs-signal trade-off. Cons: long/short perpetuals, in-sample re-optimisation, and a funding-cycle rationale that spot BTC/JPY lacks.
- **trend-only-subsignals-120m-band-edge-lot-roundup** (algorithm): cost-side, 2026-11-13 lead #2. It combines the best spacing with band-edge sizing, plus a lot round-up that fixes band-edge's sub-minimum orders at 50k.
- **trend-only-subsignals-120m-slow-trend-periods** (algorithm): internal pattern. It doubles the trend periods to match the 2h cadence, because the trend family's gross edge has been window-dependent.
