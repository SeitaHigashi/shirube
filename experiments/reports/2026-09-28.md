  783 → 72, fees 29,233 → 6,239 JPY.

At 500,000 JPY this is slightly below 7200/7200 on 2026-09-25 (+1.69% / +3.00, a different window), so
halving the action spacing gave back a little. It still trails its own static mix by −4.07 / −3.21

capitals.

### `intermediate-reversion-horizons`: inert at 50k, partial benefit at the diagnostics

It also made 1 trade at 50,000 JPY. The tiny Sharpe and DD differences come only from the opening buy's
size (exposure 0.485 vs 0.523), not from any trading. At 500,000 JPY it cut trades 783 → 441 and
lifted Sharpe −17.36 → −7.46, which falls between the baseline and 2026-09-25's
`longer-reversion-horizons`. That is a clean monotone dose-response in the reversion horizon, but the
result stays negative at every capital that trades.

### `trend-family-beyond-5h-horizons`: a clear negative

It is the only variant that traded at 50,000 JPY (100 trades). It lost −1.12% with Sharpe −1.40 against
the static mix's +4.15% / +6.52, and it was worse than baseline at both diagnostic capitals (Sharpe
−20.54 and −14.00). Lengthening only the trend periods made the trend and reversion families disagree
for longer, which produced wider target swings and more lot-clearing deltas. The paper's
superdiffusion claim does not survive shirube's composite at quarter trend weight. Combined with
2026-09-21 and 2026-09-22, the trend family has now tested negative at short, inverted, half-weight and
beyond-5h horizons.

## New hypotheses generated this run

`CANDIDATES` was empty (all 58 files were already registered), so three new hypotheses were generated:

- **`two-hour-block-hourly-spacing`** (parameter, internal; the mandatory cost hypothesis):
  `two-hour-block-two-hour-spacing` (7200/7200) was the best result at every capital on 2026-09-25 but was
  rejected only on trade count (11/81). 2026-09-22 showed that at 50,000 JPY, hourly spacing *raises*
  trades through lot-floor accumulation. This keeps the two-hour hold and halves the spacing to try to
  clear the 50% trade-count bar.
- **`intermediate-reversion-horizons`** (parameter, internal): rsi 60 / bollinger 90 sits between the
  baseline (42/60) and `longer-reversion-horizons` (90/120). The longer version collapsed to 1 trade at
  50,000 JPY on 2026-09-25, so this tests the nearer value.
- **`trend-family-beyond-5h-horizons`** (parameter, literature): arXiv:2402.11930 finds BTC trending
  only beyond ~5h, while every trend-family period (SMA 200, EMA 100, MACD 12/26/9 one-minute bars)
  sits below that. This moves them to SMA 1440, EMA 720 and MACD 360/780/270.
