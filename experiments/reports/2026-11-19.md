## New hypotheses generated this run

- **trend-only-subsignals-120m-half-band-interior-rebalance** (algorithm): cost-side, literature-motivated (Holden & Holden 2013). Rebalance to an interior point (threshold/2) because the lot floor acts like a fixed cost. It targets the drift-triggered one-lot top-ups seen on 2026-11-16. Pros: matches the observed failure mode. Cons: continuous-time single-asset model, and the threshold/2 value is a heuristic.
- **trend-only-subsignals-60m-band-edge-lot-roundup** (algorithm): cost-side, internal pattern. 120m band-edge + round-up missed the trade-count ratio by 2 trades on 2026-11-16, so this doubles the rebalance opportunities.
- **trend-only-subsignals-120m-slow-trend-band-edge-lot-roundup** (algorithm): cost-side, 2026-11-16 lead #2. It combines the two independent improvements (slow trend periods and band-edge sizing).
