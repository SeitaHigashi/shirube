### The block/spacing curve turns over past 8h

24h/24h is clearly worse than the 8h-block variants at both diagnostic capitals (−2.03 vs −0.25 Sharpe
deficit at 500,000 JPY). This is the first evidence of the interior optimum Ekren et al. predict: the
deficit stopped narrowing between 8h and 24h. 8h/8h itself was not rerun on this window, so the peak
is somewhere around 8h but not pinned down.

### `eight-hour-block-hourly-spacing-intermediate-reversion`: closest to static yet

At 5,000,000 JPY it is −0.057 Sharpe and −0.02 pp return behind its static mix, the smallest deficit
this pipeline has measured at a diagnostic capital, at a lower drawdown than buy-and-hold (2.43% vs
4.55%). It still does not beat static. Hourly drift correction within a held block did not hurt and
probably helped relative to the slower 8h/8h-type variants, but on a down window with 17 trades it is
weak evidence.

### `eight-hour-throttle-longer-reversion`: longer horizons did not help

Lengthening RSI/Bollinger to 2h/4h under an 8h throttle gave a −1.85 deficit at 500,000 JPY, much
worse than the 8h-block/1h-spacing variant using rsi60/bb90. Together with the unthrottled
`longer-reversion-horizons` rejection (2026-09-25), there is no sign that the reversion family
improves past 1-1.5h.

## New hypotheses generated this run

`CANDIDATES` was empty (all 67 existing files were already registered), so three new ones were
generated:

- **`daily-block-daily-spacing-intermediate-reversion`** (parameter, internal; mandatory cost
  hypothesis): extends the block/spacing curve past 8h/8h (2026-10-04's best static deficit) to find the
  interior optimum predicted by Ekren et al.
- **`eight-hour-block-hourly-spacing-intermediate-reversion`** (parameter, literature): holds the 8h
  composite but rebalances hourly. This turns each block into a constant-mix allocation, the mechanism
  Willenbrock credits for the rebalancing premium. It also aims to keep more trades than 8h/8h.
- **`eight-hour-throttle-longer-reversion`** (parameter, internal): lines the reversion horizon (rsi120
  / bb240) up with the 8h hold, instead of sampling 1-1.5h noise once per block.
