**What each hypothesis showed:**
- **Band-edge sizing works on cost but costs some gross value.** It cut 500k traded volume by about two thirds (3.89M vs 11.55M JPY) and fees from 11.0k to 4.3k JPY, which halved the net deficit (−4.27 → −2.08 pp). Stopping at the band edge cost ~0.57 pp of gross timing value (−0.62 vs −0.05 pp at zero cost). At 50k, 102 rebalances cleared the threshold but the band-edge order size (|delta| − 0.15) fell under the lot floor.
- **120m spacing was the best of the trend-only doses on this window**, both gross (+0.76 pp zero-cost) and net (−1.96 pp). Trades fell from 95 to 60. Still not net positive: costs took ~2.7 pp at 500k (fees 1.51 pp, the rest slippage).
- **Trend-only's gross edge did not reproduce at 60m.** It was +2.98 pp (10m, 2026-11-07), then +2.06 pp (60m, 2026-11-10), and is −0.05 pp (60m) today. Three windows in, the trend-family gross edge looks window-dependent rather than stable.
- **The constant-mix timing null finally fired, at 5M only.** It made 14 trades at 5M, with zero orders below the minimum. It is flat against the static mix (−0.03 pp net, −0.01 pp at zero cost), so this window has no rebalancing premium to capture. At 50k and 500k it made 1-2 trades, which is not a measurement.
- **Compared at 5M, the TA timing adds nothing over a drift rebalance here.** At zero cost the 60m trend-only code scores −0.20 Sharpe vs its static mix, against +0.02 for the constant mix.

Suspected defects and caveats, not fixed:
- **The 50k promotion rule cannot currently measure anything.** For two runs in a row the 50k baseline has made 3 and then 1 trade. That makes it a static mix, so every variant that trades is rejected on Sharpe, while at 500k and 5M the same variants beat the baseline by 8-13 pp. This is the doc's "floor moves with the BTC price" caveat.
