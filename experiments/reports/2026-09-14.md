### The trade-count criterion is now the binding constraint

This is the run's substantive result and it supersedes every individual verdict. With the
promotion capital finally non-degenerate, two independent mechanisms cleared **both**
strategy criteria at **every** capital and were rejected solely for trading less:

| | Sharpe Δ @50k | MaxDD Δ @50k | Sharpe Δ @500k | MaxDD @500k | trade ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ewma-smoothed-signal-wallclock-30m | +2.550 | −1.06pp | **+23.572** | 16.47% → 4.91% | 4% / 8% |
| subsignal-agreement-gate | +2.703 | −1.62pp | **+18.065** | 16.47% → 7.62% | 2% / 26% |
| post-trade-cooldown-90m (diagnostics only) | — | — | **+17.456** | 16.47% → 8.00% | 25% |

Every `compare-backtest` rejection above reads the same way: two Pros, one Con, and the Con
is always `trade count collapsed to N% of baseline`. Note the third row: `post-trade-cooldown-90m`
was genuinely refuted at the primary capital, yet at 500k it too shows a large Sharpe gain
rejected on trade count. **All three of today's mechanisms work by trading less, and the
promotion rule's `MIN_TRADE_COUNT_RATIO = 0.5` cannot promote anything that does.**

That is not an argument that the rule is wrong — it exists to stop a variant "winning" on a
handful of lucky fills, and `ewma-smoothed-signal-wallclock-30m`'s 2 trades at 50k (both
buys, win rate 0.0) is exactly the degenerate sample it was written to catch. But it is now
in direct tension with the doc's own standing finding. The doc says the strategy loses to its
own exposure held statically, i.e. that its *turnover* is the problem; the promotion rule
then refuses any candidate that reduces turnover by more than half. **The loop as currently
specified is structurally incapable of promoting the only class of change that has ever
improved its metrics.** This is a human decision and was not taken unilaterally — the rule
was invoked exactly as written and `backtest::report::compare` was not modified. Three
possible resolutions, for a human to choose between:

1. Raise the promotion capital so the lot floor stops dominating what "a trade" means at 50k.
   This has been the standing lead since 2026-09-11 and would not, by itself, fix the tension.
2. Replace the trade-count *ratio* with an absolute floor (e.g. "candidate must make at least
   30 trades in the window"), which still rejects a 2-trade sample but permits a 771 → 199 cut.
3. Accept that a candidate collapsing toward its own static mix is a real answer — the
   strategy has no edge to give up — and change what the pipeline is optimizing.

### What each hypothesis actually established

**`subsignal-agreement-gate` answers 2026-09-13's lead #4, and that is worth more than its
verdict.** Instrumented over 15,286 candles, the gate rejected 13,117 (85.8%). On **all 2,998
candles where all five sub-signals expressed an opinion, they never once reached 4-of-5
agreement**; the 8,230 four-vote candles were never unanimous. Every single pass came from a
sparse 1–3 voter candle. So the composite's narrow [0.3359, 0.6548] band is **genuine
cancellation**, not a small real edge being diluted: the reversion family (RSI, %B) and the
trend family (SMA/EMA/MACD cross) are near-mechanically opposed, because price above its
moving averages is usually also high in its Bollinger band, making the split systematically
2-vs-3 or 3-vs-2. Gating did not widen the realized band — it narrowed it
([0.2655, 0.7212] → [0.2876, 0.6975] plus an 85.8% point mass at exactly 0.5), because the
most extreme points were themselves the disagreeing ones. Recorded and deliberately **not**
retuned mid-run: `AGREEMENT_MIN_FRACTION = 0.8` under `majority >= 0.8 * n_votes` demands
unanimity at n = 2, 3 and 4 (3-of-4 = 75% fails), so the gate was far stricter than "4 of 5"
on sparse candles.

**`ewma-smoothed-signal-wallclock-30m` fixes the live-transferability blocker that disqualified
its predecessor.** The decay is a function of elapsed wall-clock time
(`0.5^(dt/1800)`), asserted cadence-invariant by direct test: a 0→1 step reaches 0.5 ± 1e-9
after 1800 s whether delivered as 30 × 60 s updates or 7,200 × 250 ms updates. The 2026-09-13
bar-indexed version would have had a ~7.5 s effective half-life on the live ~4 Hz engine; this
one means 30 minutes in both. Mechanism measured: signal range −35.7% (0.318991 → 0.205012),
sd −43.7% (0.073610 → 0.041478), and bar-to-bar allocation-target moves of at least
`allocation_threshold` went **228 raw → 0 smoothed**. Caveat carried forward honestly: at 50k
it makes 2 trades (both buys) and `excess_return_vs_static_mix_pct` goes −1.683 → −0.046 — it
approximates its own static mix again, so the primary result is "buy once and hold", not
better timing. The 500k/5M runs, with 33/31 buy/sell at ~4.6 trades/day, are the informative
ones.

**`post-trade-cooldown-90m` is the one genuine refutation, and its mechanism is the surprise.**
A *suppression-only* gate **raised** the trade count at 50k (48 → 84). That is the lot floor,
not a bug: average executed trade size is pinned at the 0.001 BTC minimum in both runs
(13,339 vs 13,342 JPY), so suppressing rebalances lets allocation drift accumulate and the
pending delta clears the floor more often — `orders_below_min` fell 6,203 → 4,376, 1,827
previously-dropped rebalances became 36 extra fills, traded volume doubled, and `fee_drag_pct`
went 1.70 → 2.83. Sharpe −5.849 → −11.549 and drawdown 5.67% → 8.07% follow directly. This is
the same sign-flip-between-capitals shape `rebalance-spacing-15m` showed on 2026-09-12, and
the same cause. It also closes no part of the static-mix gap at any capital
(`sharpe_minus_static_mix` −8.41 at 50k against the baseline's −2.72).

  Forcing `ta_normalized = 0.5` still returns `Some(...)`, which still updates `sticky_target`
  and could in principle command a rebalance *toward* 50% that the ungated logic would not
  have made. Empirically it only ever suppressed on this window (48 → 1 at 50k, 771 → 199 at

## New hypotheses generated this run

All 31 hypothesis files on disk were already in `experiments/tried.json` (`CANDIDATES` was
**empty**), so step 3 generated the maximum of 3, and all three were tried the same run. They
were committed and pushed (`0ce7d21`) **before** the agents were launched, per 2026-09-13's
operational note. No candidates carry over to tomorrow.

- **`ewma-smoothed-signal-wallclock-30m`** (algorithm): EWMA on the composite signal with the
  half-life expressed in **wall-clock seconds** rather than bars. Two motivations, both from
  2026-09-13: the bar-indexed version produced the largest improvement this pipeline had ever
  measured (500k Sharpe −30.58 → −6.17) but could not be scored because the primary baseline
  made 1 trade; and it was explicitly recorded as "non-transferable to live until that cadence
  is resolved", which a time-decayed formulation fixes outright.
- **`subsignal-agreement-gate`** (algorithm, literature-motivated): require 4-of-5 directional
  consensus among the sub-signals before the allocation may leave neutral. Motivated by
  2026-09-13's lead #4 — the composite never leaves [0.3359, 0.6548] and the compression is in
  the five-sub-signal mean — and by the FinRL ensemble paper's majority-vote aggregation (see
  "Literature reviewed"). Deliberately an *information* filter, not a gain change, so it is not
  a repeat of the four weighting variants already tried, none of which can suppress a
  disagreeing point at all.
- **`post-trade-cooldown-90m`** (algorithm): 90-minute post-fill cooldown. Re-tests a mechanism
  recorded 2026-09-13 as untestable rather than refuted, at a length chosen to actually bind at
  the primary capital — at 3.43 trades/day a 30-minute window would have been near-inert there,
  reproducing exactly the "identical report" non-result the doc warns about.

**Leads for the next generation cycle**, in rough order of promise:

1. **Resolve the trade-count criterion versus the standing no-gross-alpha finding.** See "The
   trade-count criterion is now the binding constraint" above. This is a human decision and it
   supersedes everything below: until it is settled, any turnover-reducing hypothesis — the only
   class that has improved the metrics — is unpromotable by construction, and the loop will keep
   generating rejections that read as two Pros and one Con.
2. **Merge PR #23.** `dev` does not compile. Everything else is downstream of this.
3. **Test a looser consensus rule on the agreement gate.** Today's run proved the five
   sub-signals never reach 4-of-5 agreement, so 0.8 is a null gate at n = 5 by construction, and
   `majority >= 0.8 * n_votes` silently demands unanimity at n = 2, 3, 4. A `ceil`-style "n−1 of
   n" rule, or 0.6, would test the mechanism rather than its accidental strictness.
4. **De-duplicate the five sub-signals into two family votes.** The measured cause of the
   cancellation is that the reversion and trend families are near-mechanically opposed, and five
   correlated votes overstate their independence. Collapsing to one reversion vote and one trend
   vote, and deciding when they disagree, is a more honest expression of the same idea.
5. **Port the wall-clock time-constant pattern to any future temporal hypothesis.** Demonstrated
   working today; it removes the ~240× live/backtest distortion for the hypothesis class that
   has produced this pipeline's best results.
6. **`sentiment_weight` is untestable by construction in this harness**; **`rebalance_interval_secs`
   is now testable** (PR #21 merged); the volatility-scaling lead stays closed, per 2026-09-10.
