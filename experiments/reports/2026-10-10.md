### The 8h peak holds on a second window

With spacing held at 1h and the reversion horizons held at rsi60/bb90, the static-mix deficit at
500,000 JPY is: 4h **−1.64**, 8h **−0.35** (control), 12h **−1.00**. At 5,000,000 JPY it is −1.22,
−0.14 and −0.89. The 8h block remains the interior optimum that 2026-10-07 first found. Both flanks
are now measured with the spacing confound removed.

### A narrower band made things worse, not better

The Davis–Norman band (0.04) let the hourly drift corrections fire: 34 trades vs 19 for the 0.1
control. Those extra 15 trades cost Sharpe at both diagnostic capitals (−0.72 vs −0.35 deficit @500k,
−0.40 vs −0.14 @5M). The trades were also not a fee-tier artefact: fees rose 31% @500k for a worse
gross result. Rebalancing to the held target within an 8h block does not earn a rebalancing premium on
this window. Shorter-horizon reversion at 1h, if it exists here, is smaller than the ~0.13% round-trip
cost. Together with the 2026-10-07 result, this suggests that the 1h spacing helps (if at all) through
the occasional large drift correction, not through frequent small ones. The paper's own Cons (a
moving target, trading to the target rather than to the band edge) are the likely reason.
