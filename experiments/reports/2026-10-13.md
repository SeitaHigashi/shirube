At 5,000,000 JPY, band 0.15 gives **+0.004**. That is the first non-negative static-mix gap measured
for any throttle variant in the 2026-10-07..10-13 series. It is effectively zero, and it comes from
one window and 14 trades, so it is "matches static" at best, not evidence of alpha. Going from 0.15
to 0.20 cut only 5 trades but lost 0.9 of Sharpe. That looks like path dependence in which
block-boundary target changes happen to clear the band, more than a smooth cost curve. Treat the
optimum's location as noisy.

−0.51 / −0.35 (control), 12h −1.00 (10-10). The peak sits at 8h on three windows now, and 6h is
clearly on the lower flank, not a second peak.
