**Read these carefully. Most of the purged hypotheses carry a stale `trading_config`** written against
the old defaults: zone 0.2/0.8 and MACD 52/104/18. The current baseline is zone 0.1/0.9 and MACD 12/26/9.
Their deltas therefore mix the mechanism with a config change. Six configs share zone 0.2/0.8 + MACD
52/104/18 and differ only in `allocation_threshold` (0.05-0.25) or in code that is already on `dev`. They
are **economically identical at 50k** (83 trades, Sharpe −15.525), which is the lot-floor inertness the
doc warns about. Their 50k verdict is a verdict on zone 0.2/0.8, not on the threshold. They come apart at
the diagnostic capitals, from −7.68% (threshold 0.25) to −28.61% (threshold 0.05) at 5M.

Notable results:
- **`wide-zone-band` — promoted (PR #34).** Its zone matches the baseline, so it is effectively "MACD
  52/104/18 instead of 12/26/9" and reverts `classic-macd-periods` (promoted 2026-09-15). The margin is
  small: +0.51 Sharpe, −0.11 pp drawdown and 2 fewer trades. **The sign flips across capitals**: it is
  slightly worse at 500k (−16.69 vs −16.41) and at 5M (−13.36 vs −13.05). Flagged in the PR for human
  judgement.
- **`rebalance-spacing-15m` — near-miss.** Sharpe +2.33, but drawdown worsened by 0.48 pp. It is the
  strongest pure-config improvement at the diagnostic capitals: Sharpe −21.2 vs −31.4 at 500k and −16.7
  vs −24.6 at 5M. It differs from the baseline only in `rebalance_interval_secs: 900`, plus MACD
  52/104/18 and zone 0.2/0.8.
- **`trend-weight-half` — near-miss.** Sharpe +2.30, but drawdown worsened by 0.91 pp. Against its own
  config control (the 83-trade zone-0.2/0.8 group, threshold 0.10), raising the trend weight from 0.25 to
  0.5 lifts 50k Sharpe from −15.53 to −10.10 and 500k Sharpe from −47.6 to −28.4.
- **`trend-weighted-subsignals`** (trend 1.0 / reversion 0.25, the mirror of what `dev` ships) is
  rejected at 50k. It is nonetheless **the best result of the run at both diagnostic capitals**:
  −9.35% / Sharpe −17.8 at 500k and −7.52% at 5M. Against its config control (threshold 0.15) it improves
  500k from −18.05% to −9.35% and 5M from −14.55% to −7.52%. Taken with `trend-weight-half`, this is
  consistent evidence on 1-minute bars that **`dev`'s reversion-heavy weighting (adopted on hourly
  CoinGecko data) is on the wrong side**.
- **`cost-aware-rebalance-filter`** has no trades at all at any capital. Its gate cannot pass any delta
  that `allocation_threshold` 0.1 lets through (maximum expected move 0.000455 < 0.005·|delta| for |delta|
  ≥ 0.091). It "improves" Sharpe to 0 by staying in JPY, and the 0/66 trade-count criterion rejects it.
  This is a calibration failure, not a test of the idea.
- **`realized-vol-scaled-exposure` and `volatility-scaled-zone-width`** were calibrated for 1h bars. On
  1m bars the vol-scaling factor sat at its 1.5 cap on 100% of bars, and the zone-width map acts as a
  permanently narrower band. **These are not fair tests of volatility scaling.**
- **`min-notional-aware-rebalance`** rounds sub-minimum orders up to 0.001 BTC. At 50k that overshoots the
  target by more than the threshold, so it churns: 1,045 trades, 14 circuit-breaker trips, −54.8%. The
  change cannot fire at 500k/5M.

Sign flips between capitals: only `wide-zone-band`, which is better at 50k and worse at 500k and 5M.

  except for its doc section.
- **`backtest-data push` returns 403** (session-type policy). The hole is now ~9.3 days and grows daily.

Suspected defects (reported by worktree agents, not fixed):
- **Live `fee_pct()` is hardcoded** (`src/exchange/bitflyer/rest.rs` returns 0.0015 with real API keys).
  The live client never sees its real commission tier, while `MockExchangeClient` does, so live and
  backtest can disagree on anything fee-dependent. **Touches live trading.** The fix would read
  `get_trading_commission`.
- **The simulator ignores `send_order` errors** (`let _ =` in `Simulator::run`). An order that passes
  sizing but fails at fill time, for example a buy affordable at mid but not at ask plus fee, shows up in
  no counter.
- **`rolling-vol-scaled-exposure` / `realized-vol-scaled-exposure`**: the live `SignalEngine` builds
  `IndicatorPoint` without close history, so any feature computed only in `compute_indicators()` is None
  in live trading. A promoted indicator-feature hypothesis would silently be a no-op live. This is an
  architectural gap worth a human look before the next such hypothesis.
- Cosmetic: `total_fees_jpy` prints `-0.0` on a zero-trade run.
