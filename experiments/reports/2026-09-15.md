This supersedes the individual verdicts. Two independent mechanisms both **widened** the
composite's realised band, both **raised** turnover by roughly 8×, and both made every
strategy metric dramatically worse at every capital:

Both agents instrumented **both** formulas per call over the identical 15,417 evaluated

2026-09-14 measurement to four decimals, independently, in two separate worktrees. That
cross-validates the harness and means the widening figures are trustworthy.

Read alongside `ta-weight-085-signal-amplitude` (2026-09-13: amplified the composite, Sharpe
−14.034, drawdown +7.82pp), the picture is now consistent across four experiments: **band
width itself is the damage mechanism.** Whichever way the signal points, a wider band means
more exposure swing, more turnover and more fee drag against a series the signal cannot time.
Amplifying it loses. Inverting it loses *more*. De-correlating its votes so it can reach
further loses too. The only changes that have ever improved the metrics are the ones that
made the strategy trade less — and those are exactly what the promotion rule's
`MIN_TRADE_COUNT_RATIO` rejects (2026-09-14's finding, unchanged).

### What each hypothesis actually established

**`contrarian-trend-subsignals` is the informative negative, and its mechanism was confirmed
even as its idea was refuted.** The prediction was that inverting the three trend sub-signals
would make all five agree instead of oppose, widening the band — and it did, by 1.66×
([0.3359, 0.6548] → [0.2410, 0.7712], sd 0.0736 → 0.1201), with turnover rising 24 → 208 at
50k and 789 → 1439 at 500k. So the composite is **not** "informative with the wrong sign":
flipping it made everything worse at every capital. Both the *gain* of the trend family
(three prior hypotheses) and now its *sign* have been tested. What has never been tested is
whether the composite should drive exposure **at all**, versus a damped or static allocation.
One fixture outside `engine.rs` needed adapting and is flagged rather than buried:
`simulator.rs::run_with_drawdown_limit` built its pre-crash BTC position from a *rising*
price series, which reads bearish under the inversion, so it was flipped to a falling series;
all three assertions (`circuit_breaker_trips >= 1`, `orders_rejected >= 1`, the 0.99-limit
inert case) are unchanged.

**`family-vote-aggregation` answers 2026-09-14's lead #4 with a number.** Collapsing the five
sub-signals to two family votes makes the realised reversion:trend ratio exactly 4:1 instead
of the 2.67:1 that three correlated trend votes at weight 0.25 against two reversion votes at
1.0 actually produce. The band widened, but only **+8.8%** in width and +19.4% in sd, and
essentially all of it on the bullish side (max 0.6548 → 0.6812; min 0.3359 → 0.3342). So the
cancellation is only *partly* a vote-counting artifact — removing the inflation does let the
composite reach further, but a mathematically exact 4:1 ratio still leaves it inside roughly
±0.17 of neutral. The two families really are near-mechanically opposed, as 2026-09-14
concluded. Lead #4 is now closed: de-duplicating the votes is not the fix.

**`classic-macd-periods` was promoted, and its own prediction was wrong.** It predicted a
faster MACD would raise turnover; at the deciding capital turnover *fell* (20 vs 24 trades,
266k vs 322k JPY volume), so the trade-count floor was cleared by margin rather than by the
predicted mechanism. Both diagnostic capitals are slightly *worse* and agree in sign (500k
Sharpe −30.468 vs −30.457; 5M −24.143 vs −24.123) — unthrottled by the lot floor, the faster
MACD trades slightly more (792 vs 789/791) and loses slightly more. Per the doc those have no
veto and the variant is promoted regardless, but the divergence is recorded so a human can
see it. The improvement has the shape "trade slightly less in a down market", and
`sharpe_minus_static_mix` improves only −1.315 → −1.149, staying firmly negative.

### Suspected defects found and deliberately not fixed

- **The warmup request is systematically short.** `requested 300 candles, got 226` — the DB's
  pre-window margin, not the flag, is binding. It affects baseline and every variant
  identically so no comparison is biased, but with `sma_period: 200` the earliest evaluated
  points sit on thinner history than intended. The fix is more pre-window data, which is
  gated on the frozen `backtest-data` snapshot above. Carried from 2026-09-14.
- **The lot-size floor makes the promotion capital a quantised measurement.** 6,109 of the
  baseline's 6,133 intended rebalances died under the 0.001 BTC minimum (99.6%). The 500,000
  JPY diagnostic runs `orders_below_min: 0` and is the cleaner read of any mechanism; it
  agreed with the 50k verdict on all three hypotheses this run, which is reassuring but not
  guaranteed. This is documented behaviour, not a bug — but it is why the 2026-09-11 lead
  about raising the promotion capital keeps recurring.
- **No backtest can exercise the sentiment path.** `Simulator::run` hardcodes `&[]` for news,
  so `combined = ta_w·ta_norm + (1 − ta_w)·0.5` and `sentiment_weight` is a pure gain knob.
  Carried unchanged from 2026-09-13 and 2026-09-14; still a design decision for a human.
- **A failed `send_order` is still invisible in every counter.** The live engine discards
  `send_order`'s `Result`. Carried from 2026-09-13; no competing diff blocks it any more, so
  it remains a clean candidate for its own enabling PR.
- **The live/backtest cadence divergence is still unfixed.** `SignalEngine::run` republishes
  every 250 ms while the backtest evaluates once per 60 s bar, so any bar-indexed time
  constant means something ~240× different live. The wall-clock remedy demonstrated on
  2026-09-14 remains the pattern for any future temporal hypothesis.
- **Stale remote branch `origin/worktree-agent-a626114efaa265bd1`** is still present and
  appears to belong to no hypothesis. Not deleted — removing someone else's remote branch is
  not this pipeline's call. Carried from 2026-09-14.

## New hypotheses generated this run

All 34 hypothesis files on disk were already in `experiments/tried.json` (`CANDIDATES` was
**empty**), so step 3 generated the maximum of 3, and all three were tried the same run. They
were committed and pushed (`d5f23b6`) **before** the agents were launched, per 2026-09-14's
operational note. No candidates carry over to tomorrow.

- **`family-vote-aggregation`** (algorithm): collapse the five correlated sub-signals into one
  reversion vote and one trend vote so `REVERSION_SUBSIGNAL_WEIGHT`/`TREND_SUBSIGNAL_WEIGHT`
  realise their intended 4:1 ratio instead of the 2.67:1 that three trend votes against two
  reversion votes actually produce. Motivated by 2026-09-14's lead #4 and its measurement that
  the five sub-signals never reach 4-of-5 agreement over 2,998 fully-expressed candles.
- **`contrarian-trend-subsignals`** (algorithm, literature-motivated): invert the three
  trend-family sub-signals. Motivated by arXiv:2608.21888's finding that crypto short-horizon
  dynamics are reversal-dominant and that 'the signal lives in signs, not magnitudes', plus
  the internal observation that the 500k baseline runs 789 trades at a win rate of 0.107 —
  systematically wrong-sided rather than noisy — and that every prior trend hypothesis varied
  a non-negative *gain* and so could not have detected a *sign* error.
- **`classic-macd-periods`** (parameter): isolate the MACD triple at the canonical 12/26/9.
  Motivated by the observation that every indicator period was chosen under the pre-2026-09-09
  belief that the engine ran at 1h resolution, and that the MACD triple is the only part of
  the config never re-examined since that correction.

**Leads for the next generation cycle**, in rough order of promise:

1. **Test whether the composite should drive exposure at all.** This is now the pipeline's
   central open question and it is newly well-posed: four experiments
   (`ta-weight-085-signal-amplitude`, `family-vote-aggregation`, `contrarian-trend-subsignals`,
   and the standing benchmark deficit) agree that band width is the damage mechanism and that
   the composite has negative edge in both signs. The untested direction is *narrowing* —
   compressing the composite toward neutral by a fixed factor, so exposure tracks a damped
   version of the signal rather than the signal itself. Note honestly that this is a
   turnover reducer and therefore collides head-on with lead #2.
2. **Resolve the trade-count criterion versus the standing no-gross-alpha finding.** Carried
   unchanged from 2026-09-14 and still a human decision: the doc says turnover is the problem,
   the promotion rule refuses any candidate that halves it. Today's run did not hit the
   tension (all three variants passed the trade-count criterion, two by trading 8× more), but
   lead #1 will hit it immediately. The three options recorded on 2026-09-14 stand: raise the
   promotion capital, replace the ratio with an absolute floor, or change what the pipeline
   optimizes.
3. **Raise the promotion capital.** Standing since 2026-09-11. At 50,000 JPY the lot floor
   discarded 99.6% of intended rebalances this run; at 500,000 JPY it discards none. The
   diagnostics agreed with the primary on all three verdicts today, but the primary is
   measuring a quantised strategy and one day they will disagree on something that matters.
4. **Lead #4 from 2026-09-14 is closed.** De-duplicating the five sub-signals into two family
   votes was tested and refuted today; the cancellation is only ~9% a counting artifact. Do
   not re-propose it.
5. **A looser consensus rule on the agreement gate** (2026-09-14's lead #3) remains open but
   is now less attractive: it is a suppression mechanism, so it runs straight into lead #2's
   trade-count floor, and today's evidence says the disagreeing points are not where the
   damage concentrates — width is.
6. **`sentiment_weight` is untestable by construction in this harness**; the volatility-scaling
   lead stays closed, per 2026-09-10.
