### The block/spacing curve: still rising at 8h, still behind static

At 500,000 JPY, 8h/8h is the best throttle measured so far by `sharpe_minus_static_mix` (−0.58). Fees
fell 28,874 → 1,873 JPY (−94%). Sharpe +4.48 at 8h/8h versus +3.70 for 4h/4h + rsi60/bb90 (4h/4h alone
was not rerun on this window, so the two are not a clean dose step). The deficit to static narrows
monotonically as the strategy trades less. That is the signature of a strategy converging *toward*
its static mix, not past it. **No throttle level has yet beaten its static mix at 500,000 JPY on any
window.** The 2026-10-01 static-beating result for 2h/2h + rsi60/bb90 at 50,000 JPY (8 trades) is
still unreplicated.

### `half-hour-throttle-intermediate-reversion`: too mild

It kept 181 trades at 500,000 JPY (24% of baseline), cut fees 57%, and moved Sharpe −20.03 → −3.31.
It is still −8.38 behind static. Even this mildest throttle would keep only about a quarter of the
baseline's trades at the capital where the strategy actually trades, so it would not clear the 50%
trade-count bar either.
