**What the runs showed:**
- **Taking the spacing out did not keep 50k turnover.** At the baseline's own 60s cadence and 0.10 threshold, band-edge sizing still cut 50k trades from 134 to 13. That rules out spacing as the reason the 2026-11-22 variants collapsed. Band-edge sizing itself is the reason: the band-edge size is almost always below one lot, and the round-up fires only when the full delta clears the lot, which at ~¥11M/BTC takes |delta| ≳ 0.22.
- **At 500k the 1m variant made *more* trades than baseline (1,166 vs 824) but halved fees.** It makes many smaller orders, so the fee tier improves and volume drops ~3x. It is still far behind 15m spacing on every axis, so spacing does most of the cost work at larger capital.
- **The lot-quantized band is the 2026-09-21 lot-floor anti-throttle again, at full strength.** At 50k it turned ~2,800 refused dust rebalances into 606 one-lot trades. Win rate was 0.24, fee drag 13.2%, return −35.07%, and the circuit breaker tripped 3 times. RiskManager refused another 130 one-lot orders. At 500k and 5M the rule never fired (a lot is under the band there), so those rows equal the 1m band-edge variant. Trading the lot does not help when the signal has no gross edge to pay for it.
- **No sign flips between capitals** for the two band-edge variants. Both beat baseline at every capital. The lot-quantized variant is much worse at 50k and neutral vs the 1m variant above it.
- **The trade-count criterion is again the only thing blocking the band-edge line.** This is the 2nd run in a row where every cost-reduction variant cleared Sharpe and drawdown by +4 to +5.5 and failed only `MIN_TRADE_COUNT_RATIO`. Today's run also shows that the one way to restore 50k trade count (trading dust as lots) destroys the result. Inside the current rule there is no route left for this line at 50k.

- **dev-weights-1m-band-edge-lot-roundup** (algorithm): cost-side, from 2026-11-22 lead #2. The baseline's exact config with only the sizing changed, to separate the band-edge effect from spacing. The 2026-11-22 60m/120m variants kept only 2-4 trades at 50k.

- **dev-weights-1m-lot-quantized-band** (algorithm): cost-side, literature-motivated (Martin & Schöneborn, arXiv:1103.4934). When a gate-passing rebalance is dust, it trades exactly one lot, but only if that lands inside the band. Pros: 50k is a discrete-lot problem and the baseline refuses ~7k such rebalances per window. Cons: continuous-time model with a known target, no BTC evidence, band width not derived for shirube.
