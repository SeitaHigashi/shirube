### One of three verdicts is real; two are arithmetic

**`ta-weight-085-signal-amplitude` is the one genuine test, and it lost decisively.**
Raising `ta_weight` 0.70 → 0.85 scales the composite by exactly 1.2143, which pushed 143
rebalances through the lot floor the baseline could not clear (1 → 144 trades at 50k,
765 → 1100 at 500k). It is worse on every promotion criterion at all three capitals: Sharpe
−5.367 → −19.401, max drawdown 4.23% → 12.05%, return −3.28% → −11.41%, `fee_drag_pct`
0.08 → 4.62. A cost-dominated strategy given more turnover.

Its factual premise was verified **true in all four parts** on `dev`'s current code:
`Simulator::run` passes `&[]` for news, so `sentiment_normalized` is pinned at exactly 0.5;
`combined = 0.7·ta + 0.15` spans only [0.15, 0.85]; `apply_zone`'s saturation arms
(raw < 0.1, raw > 0.9) are therefore structurally unreachable; and the allocation target is
confined to [0.0625, 0.9375]. **But the diagnosis that premise implied is wrong, and that is
the more valuable result.** Measured over the 15,248 evaluated candles, the realized
composite never leaves **[0.3359, 0.6548]** — nowhere near either theoretical bound. The
zone's dead saturation arms and the constant sentiment term were never what compressed the
signal; the five-sub-signal weighted mean is, and it never leaves `ta_normalized ∈
[0.2655, 0.7212]`. Reweighting only dilates that same curve about its centre, buying
amplitude and cost without buying any information.

**The other two verdicts are the lot-floor artifact the doc names explicitly**, and both
agents flagged it rather than claiming a null result:

### Suspected defects found and deliberately not fixed

- **The live/backtest cadence divergence is no longer just untidy — it now blocks a class
  of hypothesis.** `SignalEngine::run` republishes `IndicatorOutput` every 250 ms and
  `handle_indicator`'s order path is ungated, so live evaluates ~240× more often than the
  backtest's one-per-60s-bar. Carried from 2026-09-12, but today it has a sharper
  consequence: **two of the three hypotheses were time constants**, and a time constant
  expressed in bars means something ~240× different live. The EWMA agent's own
  recommendation, which this report endorses: if that change is ever promoted, the
  half-life must be re-expressed in wall-clock time, or the live filter gated to one update
  per closed bar, *before* it reaches a live instance. Not fixed here — it is on the live
  order-submission path and both hypotheses' constraints forbade touching live scheduling.
- **A failed `send_order` is invisible in every counter.** Both `Simulator::run` and the
  live engine discard `send_order`'s `Result`. `MockExchangeClient::update_balance` can
  error ("insufficient JPY/BTC balance"), reachable because a buy pays `cost + fee` while
  `allocation_delta_to_order` sizes only against `total_value` — and the mock increments its
  cumulative `volume_jpy` (which drives the fee tier) *before* the balance check, so a
  failed order still advances the tier. Measured **0 occurrences** at 500,000 JPY today, so
  it is latent and distorted nothing. Not fixed because open PR
  [#22](https://github.com/SeitaHigashi/shirube/pull/22) already reworks the same file
  (30-day fee window, fill model, shared rate limiter) and a competing diff would conflict;
  it belongs in that PR or a follow-up to it.
- **No backtest can ever exercise the sentiment path.** `Simulator::run` hardcodes `&[]` for
  news. Combined with `TradingConfig::validate()` requiring `ta_weight + sentiment_weight ==
  1.0`, that makes the pair a one-parameter family in backtest — `combined = ta_w·ta_norm +
  (1 − ta_w)·0.5` — so `sentiment_weight` is purely a gain-and-offset knob and can never be
  evaluated as a signal. Any future hypothesis claiming to test "how much to trust news" is,
  in this harness, testing signal gain only. Not fixed: a real fix means replaying
  time-aligned historical sentiment out of `news_sentiments` into the simulator loop, which
  is a genuine feature needing its own backfill story and which would change the meaning of
  every historical report under `experiments/reports/`. A design decision for a human.
- **`Simulator::run` writes each run into the shared DB** via `backtest_runs().insert()`.
  Carried from 2026-09-12; mitigated this run by having every worktree agent copy `run.db`
  into its own worktree rather than share it, so no contention occurred.
- **`--warmup-candles` margin is thin on a sparse tape.** Requested 300, got 221 against
  `sma_period: 200` — clears it, unlike 2026-09-12's 177, but by 21 bars. Identical for
  every arm, so comparisons are matched. Recorded, not fixed.

## New hypotheses generated this run

All 28 hypothesis files on disk were already in `experiments/tried.json` (`CANDIDATES` was
**empty**), so step 3 generated the maximum of 3, and all three were tried the same run.
They were committed and pushed (`8b282ce`) **before** the agents were launched, closing the
race 2026-09-12 flagged. No candidates carry over to tomorrow.

- **`post-trade-cooldown-30m`** (algorithm, literature-motivated): after a fill, no new
  order for 30 minutes. Motivated by Kearns, Kulesza & Nevmyvaka (arXiv:1007.2593, see
  "Literature reviewed"), whose perfect-foresight bound says a market-order strategy with
  minute-scale holding periods has a low profit ceiling regardless of signal quality, and by
  the observation that shirube has **no** notion of a minimum holding period at all — the
  candle after a fill may reverse it. Deliberately distinct from the two adjacent rejected
  variants: `rebalance-spacing-15m` paced *evaluation* on a fixed clock whether or not
  anything traded (and at 50k that let the delta accumulate and *raised* turnover 58%), and
  `reversal-hysteresis-multiplier` raised the bar only for opposite-signed deltas and never
  blocked anything in time.
- **`ewma-smoothed-composite-signal`** (algorithm): 30-bar half-life EWMA on the composite
  signal before zone mapping. Grounded in the 500k baseline's 765 trades at win rate 0.108
  with a 380/385 buy/sell split — oscillation, not directional trading — and in the fact
  that shirube has no temporal filtering whatsoever: every bar of indicator noise becomes a
  candidate trade. It also closes a comparison left half-done on record: 2026-09-12 adopted
  Ma & Smith (arXiv:2411.07949), whose claim is that a no-trade band on an *unsmoothed*
  signal beats smoothing-then-thresholding, and tested only the band arm.
- **`ta-weight-085-signal-amplitude`** (parameter): `ta_weight` 0.70 → 0.85. Grounded in the
  measured fact that the backtest pins `sentiment_normalized` at exactly 0.5, so 30% of the
  signal's dynamic range is spent on a constant and `apply_zone`'s saturation arms are
  structurally unreachable. Distinguished from `full-ta-weight-no-sentiment` (rejected
  2026-09-08) both by value and by the doc's own note that that measurement was taken on the
  CoinGecko 1-hour series and is not comparable.
