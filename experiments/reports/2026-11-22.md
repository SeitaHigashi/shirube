**What the runs showed:**
- **Band-edge sizing turns the dev composite into an almost static mix.**
  - It cuts cost from ~13.3 pp to ~0.06-0.09 pp.
  - It is the first variant in this pipeline's history to show a **positive Sharpe vs the static mix** at 500k and 5M: +0.33 to +0.41.
  - Return is still 0.25-0.39 pp below the static mix, so this is not evidence of timing alpha.
  - Its gain is that it removes nearly all of the 40x cost penalty.
- **Partial adjustment loses to band-edge at the same cadence on every axis at 500k.** It made twice the trades, cost 3.5x the fees and had a worse gross excess. That matches the theory: under proportional costs the right rule is band-edge, not a partial move.
- **At 50k the lot floor swallows almost everything.**
  - `orders_below_min` was 7, 14 and 51. A delta that only just passes the 0.15 gate is below 0.001 BTC even at full size, so the round-up never fires.
  - This trade-count collapse is a capital and lot-size effect, not a measure of signal quality.
- **The trade-count criterion is now the only thing between this line and a promotion.** Every cost-reduction variant hits the same structural block: removing turnover *is* the improvement, and `MIN_TRADE_COUNT_RATIO` rejects it for that very reason. This is the human decision first raised on 2026-11-16.
