**Why the 50k verdicts are uninformative today:**
- Baseline and every variant made only the opening buy. Under the 0.001 BTC lot floor (~24-27% of a 50k account at ¥12.0-13.4M/BTC), no later delta cleared the minimum.

**What each hypothesis showed:**
- **Threshold 0.20 "wins" by not trading.** It made 1 trade at every capital, so it *is* the static mix. The equal-weight threshold curve therefore says the less this family trades, the better. That is the benchmark finding again, not new alpha. This is a fair test, and it closes the threshold curve on this window.
- **Trade-to-band-edge is a fair negative result.**
  - Fees fell 73% at 500k and 69% at 5M against the ref.
  - Sharpe fell 0.06 at 500k and 0.11 at 5M, because the position sits further from target.
  - Trading became one-sided (0 sells at 500k).
  - The fee saving did not pay for the tracking error. It also touches live order sizing.
- **1800s spacing is a fair negative result.**
  - It cut trades 11 → 7 and fees 28% at 500k.
  - It lost 0.14 Sharpe at 500k and 0.18 at 5M.
  - 600s remains the best spacing measured: 60/600/900 on 2026-10-28, and now 1800.
  - No anti-throttling showed up at 50k; it stayed at 1 trade.
