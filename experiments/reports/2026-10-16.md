### Band 0.175 is the first clearly positive static-mix gap

With 8h block / 1h spacing / rsi60/bb90 fixed, the static-mix Sharpe gap at 500,000 JPY is:

| Band | 10-10 window | 10-13 window | 10-16 window |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.04 | −0.72 | — | — |
| 0.10 | −0.35 | −0.51 | — |
| 0.125 | — | — | −0.51 |
| 0.15 | — | −0.20 | −0.26 |
| 0.175 | — | — | **+1.11** |
| 0.20 | — | −1.12 | — |

Band 0.175 beats its exposure-matched static mix by **+1.11 Sharpe / +0.56 pp at 500k** and **+1.40 /
+0.72 pp at 5M**. That is the first clearly positive excess this pipeline has measured for any config
at a capital where it actually trades. Treat it with caution:
- It is one window and 15-17 trades.
- 0.20 was strongly negative on the previous window. The curve still looks path-dependent: which
  block-boundary target changes happen to clear the band decides the result, and a smooth cost
  optimum does not. The 0.15 → 0.175 → 0.20 transitions should be re-measured on the same window
  before reading a peak into them.
- The absolute return is still negative (−1.33%) in a falling window (buy-and-hold −3.86%).

Band 0.15 reproduced closely across windows (−0.20 → −0.26 at 500k, +0.004 → +0.044 at 5M). That is
mild evidence that the 0.15 result is stable.

### 2h spacing is worse than 1h

At band 0.15, moving from 1h to 2h spacing worsened the 500k gap from −0.26 to −0.70, with the same
21 trades. Less frequent spacing delays the block-boundary reaction without removing any trades, so
1h stays the spacing optimum.
