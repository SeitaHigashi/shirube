### `two-hour-block-two-hour-spacing` — the throttling curve is still rising at the diagnostic capitals

At 50,000 JPY: return −0.15% → **+4.21%**, Sharpe 0.008 → **7.30**, DD 4.51 → 2.62%, fees 1,342 →
**231 JPY**, benchmark deficit −8.60 → −1.30 Sharpe. Rejected solely because 11 trades is 14% of
baseline. Direction is the **same at all three capitals** today (no sign flip): at 500,000 JPY it
returned **+1.69% / Sharpe +3.00** (72 trades, fees 6,604 vs 31,003) and at 5,000,000 JPY **+2.28% /
+3.94**. That beats 2026-09-22's 1800/3600 pair (+0.76% / +1.51 and +2.17% / +3.70), so the
diagnostic-capital curve has not peaked at two hours. It still trails its static mix by −5.62 /
−4.67 Sharpe, i.e. it is a better way to lose to holding, not a way to beat it.

### `longer-reversion-horizons` — at 50,000 JPY this is a static allocation

One trade in 14 days. With `rsi_period 90 / bollinger_period 120` the composite moves so slowly that
at 50,000 JPY nothing but the initial entry clears the lot floor; its +5.81% / Sharpe 8.63 is
essentially the static mix's (−0.004 Sharpe vs static). Not evidence about the signal. At 500,000 JPY
it cut trades 833 → 365 and lifted Sharpe −16.69 → −3.19, still negative.

### `typical-price-rsi-bollinger-inputs` — small, non-inert, negative

The agent confirmed the change was live (14,028 of 16,190 bars have high/low ≠ close; mean |ΔRSI|
0.858, mean |Δ%B| 0.779) and that `compute_btc_target` was not touched (diff: `rsi.rs`, `bollinger.rs`
only; `cargo test` 263 passed). It slightly *raised* turnover at every capital (81 → 91, 833 → 864)
and worsened Sharpe at all three. Smoothing intra-bar range through the indicator windows removed
the churn disaster of `close-location-subsignal` (−82.8%) but left no benefit. With this and
2026-09-22, lead #4 (smoothed close location) is answered negatively for the cheap form.

## New hypotheses generated this run

`CANDIDATES` was empty (all 55 files registered), so three were generated and committed first (`95949df`):

- **`two-hour-block-two-hour-spacing`** (parameter, literature + internal; mandatory cost hypothesis):
  2026-09-22 measured a monotone improvement at the diagnostic capitals from slowing the action cadence
  (baseline → block 1800 → spacing 3600 → both); this doubles both to find where it stops.
  arXiv:1510.05097 argues the optimal fixed rebalance interval lengthens when costs dominate tracking benefit.
- **`longer-reversion-horizons`** (parameter, internal): only the shorter direction of the dominant
  mean-reversion family's horizon had been tested (`reversion-horizon-15m`, −7.9%), and 2026-09-22
  showed faster inputs churn; tests the untested longer direction (rsi 90, bollinger 120).
- **`typical-price-rsi-bollinger-inputs`** (algorithm, internal): 2026-09-22 lead #4 — keep the
  intra-bar-range information but smooth it, by feeding (H+L+C)/3 into RSI and Bollinger rather than
  adding a raw sub-signal; no `compute_btc_target` change.
