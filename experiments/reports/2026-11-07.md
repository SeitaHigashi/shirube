**What each hypothesis showed:**
- **Constant mix 50 is not a measurement.** The 0.10 drift band around 50% would need BTC to move about +50% or −33% to fire, so it never did. The variant made 1 trade at every capital and *is* the static mix. The timing-null question is still open; it would need a band of about 0.01-0.03.
- **Trend-only has the largest gross timing value measured so far, and the worst net.**
  - At zero cost it beats its static mix by +2.98 pp at 500k: the ref manages +0.71 and the dev baseline +0.29.
  - It trades 201 times at 500k, so costs take about 7.1 pp.
  - The reversion family appears to be what dampens turnover in the equal-weight mix, rather than what destroys timing value.
- **Full TA weight follows the same dose response.**
  - A bigger signal amplitude adds gross timing value: +1.05 vs +0.71 zero-cost at 500k.
  - It also more than doubles turnover (27 → 60 trades), and net it loses to the ref (−0.95 vs −0.15 pp).
  - Live, it would also remove news sentiment from the composite.
- **The common thread:** every signal-strengthening change on this window raised gross alpha and lost more than that to costs. The next useful hypothesis keeps trend-only's gross signal but trades far less often. Candidates include trend-only with threshold 0.25+ or 30-60m spacing, or a cost-aware filter that only acts when the expected move exceeds the round trip.
