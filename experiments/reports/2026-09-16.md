The agent ran a threshold sweep at 50,000 JPY and the coordinator **independently reproduced it**
before recording it here:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.10 (baseline) | 39 | 5,846 | 696.32 | −7.886 |

Two corrections follow. First, the wall is **a cliff between 0.25 and 0.26**, not a smooth ramp at
~0.245: 0.25 still trades (41 trades, slightly *more* than baseline and slightly worse Sharpe), and
every value from 0.26 upward produces the identical degenerate single-trade result. So 0.30 is well
past the cliff rather than sitting on it. Second, at 0.20 the *only* field differing from the 0.10
baseline is `orders_below_min` (833 vs 5,846) — trading, fees, Sharpe and drawdown are bit-for-bit
equal. The doc's "thresholds 0.05, 0.10 and 0.15 produced byte-identical reports" and its step-1
instruction to sanity-check for a report "byte-identical to a previous day's" are therefore stale
now that `orders_below_min` exists on `BacktestReport`: the correct test is *economic* identity
(equal `total_trades` / `total_fees_jpy` / `sharpe_ratio`, with `orders_below_min` free to move). A
future agent applying the literal byte test would conclude a genuinely inert threshold "had an
effect". This is fixed in the enabling PR below.

### An implementation finding worth keeping

`damped-composite-exposure`'s agent found that the specified formula `0.5 + (x − 0.5) * factor` is
**not** an identity at `factor = 1.0` in IEEE-754: `0.5 + (0.1 − 0.5) * 1.0` rounds to
`0.09999999999999998`. A 1-ULP shift can cross a zone boundary or the `allocation_threshold` on some
candle among 15,462, so the formula as literally specified would not have guaranteed the
byte-identical default behaviour its constraints demanded. The fix (short-circuit `factor == 1.0`)
is mathematically vacuous and bitwise essential. Any future hypothesis that adds a
default-is-no-op numeric knob should short-circuit its identity case for the same reason.

- **`h6-block-held-signal`** (algorithm, literature-motivated): quantise the composite onto
  UTC-aligned 6-hour blocks, holding it constant within a block while leaving the rebalance cadence,

  1.54 / 847), a dimension shirube had never varied.

**Leads for the next generation cycle**, in rough order of promise:

1. **Resolve `MIN_TRADE_COUNT_RATIO`. This is now the binding constraint on the entire pipeline and
   it needs a human decision.** It was a tension on 2026-09-14 and 2026-09-15; today it is a
   measured blockage. Three independent mechanisms passed both strategy criteria at the deciding
   capital and all three were refused on trade count alone, and one of them
   (`damped-composite-exposure`) is `promoted: true` under the unmodified rule at *both* diagnostic
   capitals. The three options recorded on 2026-09-14 stand, and the evidence now discriminates
   between them: **raising the promotion capital to 500,000 JPY** would have promoted
   `damped-composite-exposure` today and is the option this run most supports, since the 50k
   verdicts were all decided by the lot floor rather than by the strategy; replacing the ratio with
   an absolute floor is second; changing what the pipeline optimizes is the largest change.
2. **Re-run `damped-composite-exposure` at a gentler factor.** Its own agent suggests ~0.9 might
   stay scoreable at 50k. With width ×0.75 → +10.194 Sharpe at 500k and four prior widening
   experiments on the other side, the band-width/Sharpe relationship is monotonic over five points
   and worth fitting rather than sampling blindly. Note this is worth doing *whatever* happens to
   lead #1: at 0.9 the variant may survive the trade-count floor on its own.
3. **Combine the two mechanisms that worked separately.** `h6-block-held-signal` (slower clock) and
   `damped-composite-exposure` (narrower band) attack different axes — one cuts how often the signal
   changes, the other how far it swings — and both improved Sharpe sharply at 500k. Neither crossed
   the static-mix line alone. Whether they compose or merely overlap is a cheap, well-posed test.
4. **The static-mix line has still never been crossed from below.** Today's three variants all
   converged to `sharpe_minus_static_mix ≈ +0.00075` at 50k, which is arithmetic (a single trade
   *is* a static mix), not alpha. The honest standing description of this system is unchanged.
5. **A looser consensus rule on the agreement gate** (2026-09-14's lead #3) remains open but is now
   clearly lower value: it is a suppression mechanism and would hit lead #1 immediately.
6. **`sentiment_weight` is untestable by construction in this harness**; the volatility-scaling lead
   stays closed, per 2026-09-10. Lead #4 from 2026-09-14 (de-duplicating sub-signal votes) stays
   closed, per 2026-09-15.
