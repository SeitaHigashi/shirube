**What each hypothesis showed:**
- **Hourly spacing is the better turnover dose for trend-only.** It kept ~90% of the reference's gross timing value (+2.06 vs +2.29 pp zero-cost) at 42% of the trades and 63% of the fees, halving the net deficit (−4.97 → −2.23 pp). Raising the threshold to 0.25 cut trades similarly but kept less gross value (+1.63 pp). Neither is net positive: fees still exceed the gross edge.

The window's move was smaller, so it made 1 trade at 50k and 500k, 2 at 5M. This is not evidence either way.

## Enabling changes

- None this run.

Suspected defects and caveats, not fixed:
- **The promotion rule now depends mostly on the BTC price, not the hypothesis.** The 50k baseline swung from 157 trades (2026-11-07) to 3 today with no config change. On a 3-trade baseline the trade-count criterion is trivially met and the Sharpe criterion compares against what is effectively a static mix, so any variant that trades will lose. Two runs ago the same criterion rejected everything for trading *too little*. This is the doc's "floor moves with the BTC price" caveat in its most extreme form so far.
- **The configured `allocation_threshold` is not the effective band at small capital.** Rebalances below 0.001 BTC are refused rather than rounded. This looks deliberate, but it means any drift band below ~2.5% is inert at 500k and below ~25% at 50k.
- Still open from earlier runs: the −0.02 to −0.09 pp residual between a 1-trade run and its static mix; the dashboard's false neutral (0.5) when `compute_btc_target` returns `None`; live vs backtest signal cadence; the live `SignalEngine` lacking `realized_vol`; the hardcoded live `fee_pct()`; `Simulator::run` discarding `send_order` errors; `-0.0` fees on zero-trade runs; `orders_below_min` scaling with cadence.
